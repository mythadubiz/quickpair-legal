Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quick Pair ("the app", "we") handles your information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — scores, streaks, stars, unlocked boards and tile sets, coins and settings — is stored locally on your device (and in your personal iCloud if enabled). We do not run our own servers and we do not collect this data.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your total wins and best streak may be submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can disable Game Center in iOS Settings.

Advertising (rewarded ads)

Quick Pair may offer optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch a short video to earn in-game coins). These ads are served by Google AdMob. When you choose to watch an ad, Google may collect and process device and usage information (such as device identifiers and approximate location) to deliver and measure ads, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. On iOS, personalized ads are only used with your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency; you can decline and still play the game normally.

If you purchase the VIP option, ads are removed.

Purchases

In-app purchases (coin packs and VIP) are processed by Apple via the App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Decline ad tracking via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Remove ads entirely with the VIP in-app purchase.
  • Reset local data by deleting the app.
