Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quick Pair ("the app", "we") handles your information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, streaks, stars, unlocked boards and tile sets, coins and settings) is stored locally on your device (and in your personal iCloud if enabled). We do not run our own servers, and this progress stays on your device.

Anonymous statistics and crash reports (only with your consent)

The first time you play, the app asks whether you agree to share anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports to help improve the game. If you accept, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase (game events such as a game started or won, or the shop opened) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports). This data is not linked to your identity and is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. If you refuse, Firebase is never started and no statistics or crash reports are sent. You can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings. Since version 1.5, nothing is sent to Firebase before you answer.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your total wins and best streak may be submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can disable Game Center in iOS Settings.

Advertising (optional rewarded ads)

Quick Pair offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch a short video to earn in-game coins. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Since version 1.5, the ads service starts only after you have answered the tracking prompt and the statistics screen. It then preloads an ad, so Google may collect device and usage information (such as IP address, approximate location and, if you allowed tracking, the advertising identifier) to deliver and measure ads, even if you do not watch one. Where required by law, Google's consent form is shown first. Ads are non-personalized unless you allow both tracking (App Tracking Transparency) and anonymous statistics. See Google's Privacy Policy.

If you purchase "Remove Ads", ads are no longer loaded or offered.

Purchases

In-app purchases (coin packs and "Remove Ads") are processed by Apple via the App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Refuse or withdraw anonymous statistics in the app's Settings.
  • Decline ad tracking via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Remove ads with the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase.
  • Reset local data by deleting the app.
